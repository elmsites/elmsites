Our Story

Started as a project.
Now it's full-time.

What began as a couple of teenagers in Riga building sites for fun turned into something real — a studio serving businesses worldwide.

We're a young team, and we treat that as an edge — not something to hide. We grew up online, we move fast, and we care about the work in a way that shows in every site we ship.

The start

A side project

It began in Riga with building sites for practice — learning design, code, and how businesses present themselves online. The more we built, the more we saw how many businesses had nothing, or something outdated.

The shift

Going all in

What started as practice became purpose. We turned the skills into a real studio — Elmsites — focused entirely on giving businesses websites they'd be proud of.

Now

Full-time, all-in

Today this is what we do. We work with businesses wherever they are, design and build their sites from scratch, and stick around to keep them running. Young, hungry, here to stay.
